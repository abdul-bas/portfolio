About Me

Building thoughtful digital experiences

I'm a Flutter developer passionate about building mobile and web applications that are responsive, user-friendly, and reliable. I enjoy solving problems through clean code and practical solutions.

I work with Flutter and Dart, along with Firebase, REST APIs, and JSON. I have experience with Provider, GetX, and BLoC for state management, and Hive, SQLite, and SharedPreferences for local data storage.

I also build responsive websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, with Figma for UI design and prototyping.

Alongside development, I have a strong foundation in data structures and algorithms, including arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, BSTs, heaps, graphs, recursion, searching, sorting, BFS, and DFS.

I use Git, GitHub, Postman, Android Studio, VS Code, and Figma throughout my development workflow. I'm always eager to learn, improve, and turn ideas into useful digital products.
